Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Ke'Stone IT Support ("we", "us") is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website. You can reach us at hello@kestone-it.co.za or +27 11 000 0000, 12 Rivonia Road, Sandton, Johannesburg, 2196. We also have a branch at 27 Cape Road, Newton Park, Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), 6045.

2. What information we collect

We only collect what we need to answer you and do the work you ask for:

  • Support request form: full name, email address, phone number, type of issue, urgency, preferred contact method, preferred date, service location preference and a description of the problem.
  • Contact form: name, email address, optional phone number, message type, subject and message.
  • Device and repair details you give us when you bring a device in or allow remote access.

We do not ask for ID numbers, banking details or card numbers through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide quotes.
  • To book, carry out and follow up on repairs, call-outs and remote sessions.
  • To keep records for invoicing, warranty and legal obligations.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for third-party marketing.

4. Cookies, storage and third parties

  • Browser storage: after you send a support request, a reference and a short summary are saved in your own browser (local storage) so you can look it up on the Repairs page. It is not sent to us and you can clear it in your browser settings.
  • Maps: the Contact page loads maps from OpenStreetMap (interactive map) and Google Maps (embedded maps). These services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
  • Email: the contact form prepares an email in your own email app; nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • Social links: Facebook, LinkedIn and WhatsApp links lead to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

5. Sharing your information

We share information only when necessary: with parts suppliers or manufacturers when arranging a repair, with professional advisers, or when the law requires it. Anyone we share with must protect it appropriately.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job records only as long as needed to complete the work, handle warranty claims and meet legal and tax requirements, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. During repairs we only access the data needed to diagnose the fault, and we ask for your permission before accessing a device remotely. No online system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending passwords or sensitive documents through the forms.

8. Your rights under POPIA

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop processing it. You may also object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@kestone-it.co.za and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

9. Children

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children without a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the "last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the website means you accept the updated policy.
