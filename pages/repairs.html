The process

How a repair works, step by step

  1. Tell us the problem

    Send a support request or call us. We ask what the device is, what happens and when it started.

  2. Drop-off or call-out

    Bring the device to Sandton, Randburg or our Gqeberha branch, or book an on-site visit. Remote diagnosis is possible for software faults.

  3. Diagnosis

    We run hardware, software and security checks to find the root cause, not just the symptom.

  4. Quote and approval

    You receive a plain-English explanation and a quote. Nothing is repaired until you approve it.

  5. Repair and test

    We fix the fault, test the device properly and keep your files safe throughout.

  6. Collection and advice

    You collect the device with a short explanation of what happened and how to avoid it next time.

Diagnostics

What we check

Pick a category to see what a diagnosis covers.

Hardware diagnostics

For machines that will not start, overheat, shut down or have physical damage.

  • Power, battery and charger testing
  • Drive health (SMART) and memory tests
  • Screen, keyboard and port inspection
  • Temperature, fan and cooling checks

Software diagnostics

For slow, crashing or misbehaving devices.

  • Operating system and driver errors
  • Startup programs and background load
  • Update and licence problems
  • Operating system reinstall assessment

Network diagnostics

For dropping Wi-Fi, slow internet and devices that cannot connect.

  • Router and signal strength review
  • Speed and latency tests
  • Cable, switch and printer connectivity
  • Interference and channel checks

Data & security diagnostics

For suspected infections, lost files and failing drives.

  • Virus and malware scan
  • Backup status and recovery options
  • Account and password exposure advice
  • Data recovery feasibility assessment
