1. About these terms

These terms apply to your use of the Ke'Stone IT Support website and to services we provide to you. By requesting a service you agree to them. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under South African consumer law, including the Consumer Protection Act.

2. Quotes and diagnostics

  • Prices on this website are indicative starting prices, not fixed quotes.
  • We diagnose the problem first and give you a quote. We do not start a repair until you approve it.
  • If the final cost is expected to exceed the quote, we ask for your approval before continuing.
  • If you decline a quote, we may charge a reasonable diagnostic fee, which we tell you about before the diagnosis begins.

3. Bookings and call-outs

Time slots are confirmed by email, phone or WhatsApp. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Travel charges may apply outside our usual service area and are quoted in advance.

4. Your data and backups

  • You are responsible for backing up your files before handing over a device or allowing remote access.
  • We take care with your data, but repairs, reinstalls and failing drives can cause data loss. We cannot guarantee that data can be recovered.
  • We only access files needed to diagnose and fix the fault. See our Privacy Policy.

5. Remote support

Remote sessions only start with your permission and you can end them at any time. You must be the owner of the device or have the owner's authority to allow access.

6. Payment

Payment is due when work is completed or on collection, unless we agree otherwise in writing. Parts may require a deposit. Maintenance plans are billed monthly or yearly as selected and can be cancelled with one month's notice.

7. Parts and warranty

  • Repair work carries a 30-day warranty against the same fault recurring. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty.
  • The warranty does not cover liquid or physical damage after the repair, misuse, new software problems or faults unrelated to the original repair.

8. Collecting your device

Please collect your device within 30 days of being told it is ready. After 90 days without contact we may charge storage fees or dispose of the device after giving you written notice, as permitted by law.
